# 03 Competitors, what people love, what they complain about

Checked 2026-11-25. Public pages only. Review quotes are summarised, not copied. Labels: confirmed / likely / unverified as in 01.

## What this means for us
1. **The gap in the market is real:** Garmin's own app is data-heavy and criticised for clutter and lag, has **no streak or daily ritual** in its listing, and its calendar lives mostly on the website. Nobody offers a small, private, playful "day companion" for a watch wearer. That is our lane.
2. **Privacy and no-ads are a selling point, not a footnote.** The best-loved paid habit app (Streaks, 4.8 stars) markets "no account, no ads, no tracking". We can say the same truthfully: data stays on the device.
3. **Complaints we must not repeat:** paywalls thrown at first launch (Finch), a dense RPG interface that overwhelms non-gamers and a dated look (Habitica), shaming/anxiety streaks (INSEAD research), recap features locked behind a subscription (Strava Year in Sport), lag from missing local caching (Garmin Connect iOS forum thread).
4. **What to copy as patterns (not code, text or art):** forgiving streaks with a visible, calm "rest day" (Apple rings pause, Duolingo freeze), tiny required action per day (Duolingo separated streak from daily goal), milestone moments (Duolingo Streak Society, Apple all-rings awards), recap cards that are fun to share (Strava Year in Sport), customisable themes (Streaks).

## What 18-28 year olds respond to (evidence is thin, label it that way)
- **Notification fatigue is real:** a New Zealand survey (April 2025) found 38% of Gen Z regularly feel overwhelmed or anxious from the number of notifications; a 2025 Harris poll reports 81% of Gen Z adults often wish they could disconnect more easily (likely, secondary reports: https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/BU2504/S00379.htm , https://stacker.com/stories/relationships/81-problem-why-gen-z-desperately-wants-to-disconnect-and-how-theyre-actually). Our app cannot push at all, which we can turn into a promise: **"No pings from us, ever."** The retention hook must be pull (a reason to open), not push.
- **Relevance beats volume:** an older Leanplum survey reported too many irrelevant notifications as the top reason people delete apps (Fast Company, dated; unverified for today): https://www.fastcompany.com/90399157/people-really-really-hate-push-notifications
- **Look and feel:** 2026 trend roundups point to bento-grid layouts, bold high-contrast "tactile" styling, saturated "dopamine" colour (reaction against beige minimalism), motion-rich microinteractions; neo-brutalism is debated (one source claims decline, others a revival): https://www.moburst.com/blog/top-mobile-web-design-trends/ , https://fireart.studio/blog/the-best-web-design-trends/ , https://abp.io/community/articles/ui-ux-trends-that-will-shape-2026-bx4c2kow (likely, general, not Gen Z-specific research). No source found for Gen Z preferences on privacy or widgets; do not claim any.

## Gaps and honesty
- I did not read live App Store review text for Strava, Finch or Habitica beyond what search summaries and listings returned; the complaint themes are directional.
- I found no Reddit threads reliably; Reddit access via search was weak. Treat "what Gen Z complains about" as inferred from listings, forum threads, trade articles and the INSEAD study, not from a survey of 18-28 year olds about this exact product.
